Running pace by heart-rate zone

Average pace at each HR zone, by month — every 15-second sample from every run is tagged by the zone your heart rate was actually in at that moment, so a run's warmup/tempo/cooldown segments each count toward their own zone instead of averaging into one misleading number. The first 5 minutes of each run are excluded, since heart rate lags actual effort at the start and would otherwise register as false Zone 1.
